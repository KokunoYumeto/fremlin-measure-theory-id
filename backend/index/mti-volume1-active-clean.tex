I have therefore prepared a shorter,
better-annotated, index which will, I hope, help readers to focus on particular areas.   It does not mention definitions, as the
bold-type entries in the main index are supposed to lead efficiently to these;  and if you draw blank here you should always, of course, try again in the main index.
Entries in the form of mathematical assertions frequently omit essential hypotheses and should be checked against the formal statements in the body of the work.
